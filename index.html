Meet your coach

More than just a game.

Developing stronger players by building sound chess understanding, confidence and a thoughtful approach to every position.

As a Chess Coach and FIDE Approved National Instructor, I’m dedicated to helping students improve through structured learning, focused practice and a deeper understanding of the game.

Training is designed to develop practical playing skills—from recognising tactical opportunities to planning strategically and making better decisions under tournament pressure.

My goal is to help every student become a more independent, disciplined and confident player, both on the chessboard and beyond it.

01

Opening Preparation

Understand opening principles, develop a practical repertoire and reach playable middlegame positions.

02

Tactical Calculation

03

04

Prepare for competitive play with game analysis, practical decision-making and focused practice.
